Kevin Owens

Brand Builder. Creative. Founder & Writer of The Ice Dream Factory.

Meet Kevin Owens (@kevinonearth), otherwise known as marketing’s mad scientist. Over the past decade he has managed more than $75M in ad spend across Meta, Google and TikTok for DTC, ecommerce, B2B, SaaS and consumer brands.

Kevin started in sales at State Farm before moving into paid media at a boutique agency, where he managed millions in ad spend for Emerald Expositions, the live-events company behind Digital Dealer, NY NOW and the International Contemporary Furniture Fair. He went on to lead paid social and search at Pacific Digital, running six-figure monthly search budgets for Gexa Energy and Frontier Utilities and ecommerce and content strategy for luxury bath brand Robern. As VP of Paid Media at Sola Wood Flowers, he scaled awareness and market share in the wedding-flower category.

Kevin writes The Ice Dream Factory, a newsletter and content studio on strategy and internet culture. He helps founders, brands and creators break through the noise and build demand that lasts as the internet rewrites how attention works.
